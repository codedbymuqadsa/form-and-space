05Design Process

FROM FIRSTIDEA TO FINALDETAIL.

A clear four-step rhythm keeps every project creative, collaborative and calm from the first conversation to handover.

  1. 01

    Discover

    We listen, visit the site and study how you live or work, then define a clear brief and vision.

  2. 02

    Imagine

    Sketches, models and mood studies explore bold directions before we commit to one.

  3. 03

    Design

    We refine the winning idea into precise plans, materials and details you can see and touch.

  4. 04

    Create

    We stay on site through construction so the finished space matches the idea, down to the last joint.
